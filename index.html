how it works

own the trenches.

  1. 01

    fees fall back to the line

    every trade of $TRENCH pays pump.fun creator fees. they go to the trench engine, a public wallet.

  2. 02

    the engine buys the winners

    every hour it scores the live pump.fun coins and buys a basket of the top performers that pass the safety gates.

  3. 03

    it rebalances against the market

    coins that fall off leave the basket. coins that run get trimmed back to their weight. every trade is onchain.

  4. 04

    you make the calls

    long or hedge any live coin over 15 minutes, 1 hour or 4 hours. real prices settle every call. points rank the board.

hedge ↓

points
